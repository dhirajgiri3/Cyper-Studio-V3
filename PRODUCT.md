# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

A mix, no single primary audience (confirmed):

- **Startup founders** who need a product designed and built end to end.
- **SMBs and D2C brands** wanting e-commerce or a serious digital upgrade.
- **Mid-market and enterprise teams** hiring a studio for SaaS and platform builds.

Their job on this site: decide whether Cyper Studio can build their product, then start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

The marketing and portfolio site for Cyper Studio (https://cyperstudio.in). It shows what the studio has shipped and converts visitors into leads through the contact flow (service → details → budget). Success = qualified project enquiries.

## Positioning

A product studio: a design + engineering partner that ships full products (SaaS, platforms, e-commerce, AI agents), not just websites. The proof is shipped work across logistics, e-commerce, EdTech, travel, fashion and EV infrastructure.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services offered in the contact flow: Ecommerce, EdTech, AI Agents, SaaS (`app/components/Constant/ContactCardConstant.jsx`).
- Budget bands in USD: $5K–$10K, $10K–$25K, $25K–$50K, $50K+, Not sure yet.
- Company facts stated on site: Delhi-based with global clients; 5+ years in the industry.
- Contact: hello@cyperstudio.in, +91 9569691483.
- `/about` and `/projects` are placeholders; the home page carries the whole story today.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Cyper Studio. Domain: cyperstudio.in.
- Logo assets: `public/Assets/Image/cyper-logo/`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Six case studies with named client testimonials, confirmed real and approved for public use: Product Bazaar, Helix, Blueship, Kashi Mitra Tours, Fad Fashion, Teslavolts (`app/components/Home/Sections/Our-Work/Data/ProjectCardsData.jsx`).
- Team/people photos: `public/Assets/Image/Dashboard-Section/`.
- Absent, never fabricate: additional clients, metrics or outcomes beyond what the case studies state, awards, press, certifications.

## Product Principles

1. Shipped work is the argument: let the case studies carry the proof.
2. Serve three audiences without diluting the message; any visitor should see something they recognise within one scroll.
3. Every section moves the visitor toward contact.
4. The craft of the site is itself evidence of the studio's skill, so it must run smoothly on ordinary devices.
