How it works

One parcel, Pune to Bengaluru. Four steps, all under your name.

1Book, 11:42 am

The best courier, picked for you.

HELIX compares prices across all your courier partners and picks the cheapest one that still arrives on time. On this parcel that's ₹37 less than express, and nobody had to check a spreadsheet.

2Label, 11:43 am

A shipping label with your logo.

Your logo, your colours and your own tracking number, printed on the label printer you already have.

3Missed delivery, day 2, 2:32 pm

When a delivery fails, you follow up.

Your customer gets a WhatsApp message from your brand, not the courier. They pick a new time, and the next attempt is booked before the rider is back at base.

4Payout, 11:59 pm

Cash on delivery, settled for you.

Every rupee collected is matched to its parcel, and the money reaches your bank account the next day.

How it fits together

Orders come in. Your brand goes out.

Bring in orders from your booking page, your online store or a simple spreadsheet. HELIX books each one with the right courier, tracks it to the door, and sends every update to your customer in your name.

  • Orders in: your booking page, online store, spreadsheet upload, your own software
  • Couriers: Delhivery, Blue Dart, XpressBees, Ekart and five more
  • Back to your customer, in your name: tracking page, SMS and WhatsApp, GST invoices, COD payouts

  • Your data stays yours

    Download every order, delivery scan and cash-on-delivery payment whenever you like, in standard file formats. Nothing is locked in.

  • Keep your tracking numbers

    Move your existing tracking number series and past shipment records over on day one, and take them with you if you ever leave.

  • Your prices, your profit

    Use your own negotiated courier rates or ours, and add your margin on top. Every invoice carries your GST number.

FAQ

Before you book a demo.

What does white-label mean here?

It means your customers only ever see your brand. Your logo, colours, web address and SMS sender name appear on the booking page, tracking page, shipping labels and invoices. HELIX works quietly in the background and never shows its own name.

Which couriers can I use?

Delhivery, Blue Dart, XpressBees, Ekart and five more: nine couriers at launch, with negotiated rates included. You add your own margin on top, and the invoice goes out in your name.

Do I need a tech team to use it?

No. You can book parcels one at a time or upload a spreadsheet of orders, all from your web browser. If you do have developers, they can connect your existing systems as well.

Can I keep my existing tracking numbers?

Yes. Your tracking number series moves over with you, and past shipment records can be brought in for reference. You can download everything at any time.

Who owns my customer data?

You do. Every order, delivery scan and cash-on-delivery payment belongs to you, and you can download all of it in one click.

How long does it take to get started?

On the demo call we'll show you HELIX already set up in your colours, so you can see how it fits your routes and plan a start date with us.
