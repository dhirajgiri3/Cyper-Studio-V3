Questions

What operators ask first.

What is HELIX?

HELIX is a white-label, multi-tenant logistics operating system built by Cyper Studio. It lets couriers, 3PLs, freight brokers, franchise networks and aggregators in India run their own branded shipping platform.

Will my merchants see HELIX or Cyper Studio?

No. Merchants and their customers see only your brand, your domain and your colours. HELIX appears only in your operator console.

Which carriers can I use?

HELIX compares rates across Indian carriers at booking. We share the current list of live carrier integrations on the demo call.

What shipments does it handle?

B2C parcels, B2B heavy freight and cross-border shipments, each with its own rate cards and booking flow.

How is HELIX priced?

HELIX is licensed as software, with professional services for setup and integration. Pricing depends on your volume and modules, and we share it on request.

Who owns the merchant relationships?

You do. Merchants sign up on your platform, under your brand, and HELIX never contacts them as HELIX.
