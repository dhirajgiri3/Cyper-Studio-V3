07Questions

Questions,
answered.

Will my customers ever see the HELIX name?

No. Your customers see your logo, colours, web address, SMS sender name and invoice details. HELIX only appears inside your own admin screens.

Which couriers can I use?

Nine Indian couriers covering road, air and express delivery, including DTDC, Blue Dart and Delhivery. High-volume customers can also get dedicated trucks between cities.

How does cash on delivery get paid out?

Each parcel's cash is tracked on its own: what was collected, the fee, and what's paid to you. You're paid the next day by default, and anything that doesn't match is flagged to your finance team.

Can I use my own website address?

Yes. Booking and tracking pages run on your own web address, and customer SMS come from your company name.

How quickly can I start?

Setting up your brand and couriers takes days, not months. You can book your first parcel under your own name within the week.
