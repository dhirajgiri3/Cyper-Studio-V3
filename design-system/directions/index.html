Design Lab: three directions (Gate 1)

Three style studies, not a design system. All content is Sample; no product claims, metrics or logos. Each study uses the same locked foundations (light mode only, Geist Sans + Geist Mono self-hosted and subset, hatched paper, corner brackets, mono index labels, annotated screenshot) and differs on palette, shape, density and heading setting. Contrast figures are computed by script. Pick one, blend, or bring your own idea at Gate 1.

Direction A · airy · hairline · hatched paper · brackets visible

Calm editorial-technical

Closest to the Spec's draft system. Generous whitespace, 1px hairlines, restrained cobalt accent used only for the CTA, links and annotation markers. Hatched paper and corner brackets are fully visible and carry the personality. Reads as a calm technical publication that happens to sell software.

Evidence: the only page that is both calm and fast in the set uses a plain category headline, one accent word and hairline borders (getenergy: Lighthouse 94, 136 KB JS). Hairline borders at 6 to 12% opacity recur (spacefs, eden, getenergy). An optional tone-shift on the second headline line is used by spacefs and eden. Nothing is copied.

Trade-offs

Fit for the Operator Founder, mid-range Android, 8 seconds

Direction B · compact · square · mono-forward · ink CTA · teal highlight

Precise instrument, data-dense

Operational-software feel. Tighter radii (2 / 4 / 6), stronger borders, 15px body, mono used generously for labels, indices and key-value rails. The primary button is ink, and a teal accent is reserved for focus, links and annotation markers so a CTA never competes with a marker. A faint 12-column guide shows the grid.

Research link

Evidence: a dense real data table in the first viewport was the strongest credibility signal observed (stableapp, crealo). Label | value pairs (workos-atlas) and mono labels (linear, eden) explain features compactly; crealo uses 2px radii and no shadows. The grid guide lines and key-value rail are our own construction.

Trade-offs

Densest and most 'serious', but least friendly to a first-time visitor; smaller body type (15px) is the weakest point for the Operator Founder on a mid-range Android. The visible grid is a NEW signature device that is not in the locked list, so it needs a Spec decision. Ink CTA changes the One Voice rule (the accent no longer marks the CTA).

Fit for the Operator Founder, mid-range Android, 8 seconds

Mixed. Very credible for the technical evaluator; 15px body and density are the risk on a small screen.

Direction C · warm paper · soft radii · pill CTA · vermilion accent

Warm and approachable

Humanises a serious product for owner-operators who run regional businesses. Warm off-white paper and warm greys, softer radii (10 / 16 / 28), pill-shaped CTAs, 18px body, 500-weight headlines. The vermilion accent is distinctive in a category that is almost entirely blue. Hatch and brackets are present but quieter.

Research link

Evidence: soft radii, pill CTAs and warm neutrals recur in the warmer references (handhold, getenergy, spacefs). A vermilion CTA was not observed on any of the 12 pages; accents in the set are blue, violet, periwinkle or black. Nothing is copied; palette and shapes are constructed from the locked constraints.

Trade-offs

Warmth risks reading as consumer or creative-agency, which the Spec is trying to leave behind; vermilion is also close to the Danger state colour, so error and CTA must be separated by form, not hue alone. White on the vermilion button is 5.18:1, but vermilion as link text on the warm page is 4.92:1, only just above AA. Pill buttons depart from the draft 10px radius.

Fit for the Operator Founder, mid-range Android, 8 seconds

Strong on approachability; needs care that the warmth does not read as an agency.
